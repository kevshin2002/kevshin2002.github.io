About

Hi, I'm Kevin.

I'm an electrical engineer working on localization and state estimation for autonomous systems: knowing exactly where a robot or vehicle is, using GNSS-INS, LiDAR map matching and the filter that ties them together.

From June 2025 to November 2026 I was at AI Racing Tech, competing in the Indy Autonomous Challenge, most recently as Technical Lead, Localization. Before that, I earned a B.S. in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Mathematics from UC San Diego.
